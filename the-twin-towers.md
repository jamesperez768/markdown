# Chapter 1: The Twin Towers Era (1997-2003)

The arrival of Tim Duncan marked the true beginning of the Spurs' dynastic run. After an injury-riddled season, the Spurs secured the #1 pick and selected Tim Duncan, pairing him with the already legendary David Robinson. This pairing was famously nicknamed the "**Twin Towers**."

## Key Milestones of the Era

1.  **1997:** Draft Tim Duncan, forming the Twin Towers.
2.  **1998:** Duncan wins NBA Rookie of the Year.
3.  **1999:** Win first NBA Championship (4-1 over the New York Knicks).
    * This title was won in a **lockout-shortened season**.
4.  **2003:** Win second NBA Championship (4-2 over the New Jersey Nets). David Robinson retires.

---
### The Admiral's Accomplishments

David Robinson, nicknamed "The Admiral," was a cornerstone of the franchise long before Duncan arrived.

> "The difference between a good basketball team and a great basketball team is teamwork." - *Gregg Popovich*

We must remember his most unique achievement:
* **David Robinson's Hall of Fame Credentials:**
    * NBA Champion (1999, 2003)
    * NBA MVP (1995)
    * NBA Defensive Player of the Year (1992)
    * Olympic Gold Medalist (1992, 1996)
